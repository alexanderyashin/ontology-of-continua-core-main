\section{Pinned Benchmark Dataset Manifest}
\label{sec:oc-core-1-3-domain-benchmark-manifest}

This appendix records the pinned benchmark families, held-out policies,
quantitative gates, and falsifiers that define lawful empirical promotion in
the current release. The point is not to restate raw registry fields one by
one, but to show which dataset family carries which evidential burden and what
would count as a genuine empirical failure.

\paragraph{Mathematics.}
Protocol \occode{OC13::EXECUTION::MATHEMATICS::ANCHOR_REPLAY} runs under the
hybrid-escalation evidence bar, but in practice this lane is deterministic
rather than measurement-driven. The held-out policy is
\occode{DETERMINISTIC_COUNTEREXAMPLE_AND_TRACE_REPLAY}, with a minimum of 31
strict cases. Coverage, sigma thresholds, Brier score, and ECE are therefore
not applicable. Promotion survives only if no replay divergence and no
surviving counterexample appear. Replay command:
\occode{validation/mathematics/replay.py}. No
external dataset manifest is required because the mathematics anchor closes on
strict replay and counterexample traces rather than on observational data.

\paragraph{Physics.}
Protocol \occode{OC13::EXECUTION::PHYSICS::CONSTANTS_SPECTRA_TRANSPORT} runs
under hybrid escalation and binds theorem-to-observable maps to pinned official
constants, spectral tables, and transport challenge corpora. The current
caseset contains 30 pinned cases, 20 held-out cases, and declared predictions
for all 30 benchmark rows. The quantitative gate requires full coverage, mean
absolute normalized error at most 1.0 sigma, p95 at most 2.5 sigma, maximum
error at most 5.0 sigma, and zero tail breaches. Promotion survives only if
every benchmark family remains within tolerance; any residual breach or broken
sign/order constraint falsifies the packet. Replay command:
\occode{validation/physics/replay.py}.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,nosep]
\item \occode{PHY_BENCH_001}: \occode{fine_structure_constant_residual}. Authority: \occode{NIST / CODATA constants}. Dataset: \url{https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/index.html}. Held-out policy: leave one family out for residual verification.
\item \occode{PHY_BENCH_002}: \occode{hydrogen_balmer_line_residual}. Authority: \occode{NIST Atomic Spectra Database}. Dataset: \url{https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/ASD/lines_form.html}. Held-out policy: reserve transitions not used during packet fitting.
\item \occode{PHY_BENCH_003}: \occode{transport_scaling_residual}. Authority: \occode{DOE Office of Science challenge corpora}. Dataset: \url{https://science.osti.gov/-/media/fes/pdf/workshop-reports/FES_Grand_Challenges_Report_final.pdf}. Held-out policy: hold out one transport-regime family.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Chemistry.}
Protocol \occode{OC13::EXECUTION::CHEMISTRY::THERMOCHEMISTRY_SPECTRA_KINETICS}
maps theorem packets to thermochemical, spectral, and kinetic observables
against pinned reference tables. The current caseset contains 30 pinned cases,
19 held-out cases, and declared predictions for all 30 benchmark rows. The
quantitative gate matches the physics lane: full coverage, mean absolute
normalized error at most 1.0 sigma, p95 at most 2.5 sigma, maximum error at
most 5.0 sigma, and zero tail breaches. A held-out thermochemical, spectral,
or kinetic family outside tolerance falsifies the promoted chemistry packet.
Replay command:
\occode{validation/chemistry/replay.py}.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,nosep]
\item \occode{CHEM_BENCH_001}: \occode{gas_phase_enthalpy_residual}. Authority: \occode{NIST Chemistry WebBook}. Dataset: \url{https://webbook.nist.gov/chemistry/}. Held-out policy: hold out one compound family from calibration.
\item \occode{CHEM_BENCH_002}: \occode{spectral_transition_residual}. Authority: \occode{NIST Chemistry WebBook}. Dataset: \url{https://webbook.nist.gov/chemistry/}. Held-out policy: hold out one spectral family.
\item \occode{CHEM_BENCH_003}: \occode{kinetic_or_equilibrium_residual}. Authority: \occode{DOE BES / EFRC challenge corpora}. Dataset: \url{https://science.osti.gov/bes/efrc/Research/Grand-Challenges}. Held-out policy: hold out one reaction-class envelope.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Biology.}
Protocol
\occode{OC13::EXECUTION::BIOLOGY::STATE_TRANSITIONS_AND_RESPONSE_SIGNATURES}
chooses one bounded biological lane at a time and evaluates predicted state
transitions or response signatures against open assay data. The current caseset
contains 30 pinned cases, 16 held-out cases, and declared predictions for all
30 benchmark rows. The quantitative gate requires full coverage, mean absolute
normalized error at most 1.0 sigma, p95 at most 2.5 sigma, maximum error at
most 5.0 sigma, ECE at most 0.05, and zero tail breaches. Promotion survives
only if the bounded signature family reproduces the held-out dataset family
without widening the claim boundary. Replay command:
\occode{validation/biology/replay.py}.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,nosep]
\item \occode{BIO_BENCH_001}: \occode{expression_signature_residual}. Authority: \occode{NCBI GEO}. Dataset: \url{https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/}. Held-out policy: reserve one time-series family for held-out replay.
\item \occode{BIO_BENCH_002}: \occode{state_transition_order_error}. Authority: \occode{NCBI GEO}. Dataset: \url{https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/}. Held-out policy: reserve one cell-state transition family.
\item \occode{BIO_BENCH_003}: \occode{response_onset_residual}. Authority: official open biological assay repositories. Dataset: \url{https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/}. Held-out policy: reserve one response-signature family.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Systems / Civilizational projection.}
Protocol \occode{OC13::EXECUTION::SYSTEMS::MACRO_TRAJECTORIES_AND_REGIME_SHIFTS}
evaluates K8 projection packets against pinned macro-process series with
held-out interval tests. The current caseset contains 30 pinned cases, 18
held-out cases, and declared predictions for all 30 benchmark rows. The
quantitative gate requires full coverage, Brier score at most 0.08, ECE at
most 0.05, and zero tail breaches. Promotion survives only if the declared
trajectory and regime-change observables remain inside tolerance on held-out
intervals; a missed turning point or out-of-band trajectory falsifies the
packet. Replay command:
\occode{validation/systems/replay.py}.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,nosep]
\item \occode{SYS_BENCH_001}: \occode{population_trajectory_residual}. Authority: \occode{World Bank Indicators API}. Dataset: \url{https://api.worldbank.org/v2/en/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL?format=json}. Held-out policy: leave out one interval family.
\item \occode{SYS_BENCH_002}: \occode{gdp_growth_turning_point_error}. Authority: \occode{World Bank Indicators API}. Dataset: \url{https://api.worldbank.org/v2/en/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG?format=json}. Held-out policy: reserve one held-out macro interval.
\item \occode{SYS_BENCH_003}: \occode{process_system_regime_score}. Authority: \occode{World Bank Indicators API}. Dataset: \url{https://api.worldbank.org/v2/en/indicator/EG.USE.PCAP.KG.OE?format=json}. Held-out policy: reserve one resource-intensity curve family.
\end{itemize}
