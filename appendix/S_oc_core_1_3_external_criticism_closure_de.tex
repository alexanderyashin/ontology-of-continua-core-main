\section{External Criticism Reaction and Scientific Closure}
\label{sec:oc13-external-criticism-closure}

This appendix records the v3 reaction to the Stanislav Tsukrov external review package. A criticism item is not closed merely because a response paragraph exists. It is closed only when the weakness is repaired in manuscript/release material, or when the relevant claim is visibly demoted and the remaining burden is recorded as a proof or research obligation.

\subsection{Closure invariant}

For substantive criticism, closure requires a concrete delta: source-tradition repair, theorem-label demotion, prediction claim ceiling, tuple role table, K-level falsifier criteria, release-packet update, or public acknowledgement entry. Raw feedback remains private and no canonical claim is promoted by this appendix.

\subsection{Prediction and theorem ceilings}
Prediction language is tiered by evidence. OC may state structural signatures and diagnostic classifiers where operators and observables are declared. It may state quantitative held-out predictions only where protocol, data/code route, formula or executable procedure, output, comparator, and falsifier are present. Legacy master-theorem labels are not promoted by label alone; only dependency-checked rows such as the bounded Theorem A route carry theorem force.

\subsection{Tuple and hierarchy limits}
The tuple is treated as an interface with primitive and derived roles. Minimality, completeness, exact K-level necessity, and higher-order functional promotion are release-bounded until their proof gates pass. The current release is closed by evidence rows, replay gates, theorem-fate discipline, and explicit extension boundaries, not by pretending unfinished proofs have been solved and not by lowering a claim as a closure mechanism.
