\label{sec:oc-core-1-3-toe-support-dossiers}

The main unified-synthesis section remains the canonical synthesis unit in the
numbered Core~1.3 sequence of the manuscript.
The material gathered here is retained only as level-wise evidence support: it
keeps the K-anchored proof, empirical, falsifier, and packet dossiers
available inside the flagship volume without repeating the synthesis-section
introduction, release gate, or synthesis prose.
Section~\ref{sec:oc-core-1-3-toe-synthesis} uses the generated synthesis
surface and labels its K-level subsections as
\occode{sec:oc-core-1-3-toe-k*}. The dossier files included here are included
exactly once in the English platinum master, through this appendix wrapper,
and carry separate \occode{subsec:toe-k*} support labels. Compatibility files
that also mention \occode{content/toe/toe_k*} are not input by the English
platinum master. A build variant must therefore choose either the compatibility
synthesis master or this appendix wrapper, never both.
The full DFS compatibility stream \occode{content/_auto_core_inputs.tex} still
names \occode{content/toe/toe_master.tex} for compatibility auditing, but the
platinum master does not input that compatibility synthesis master. The only
rendered level-wise synthesis dossier leaves in this build are the files included
below. The separate constants-and-parameters apparatus remains in
Appendix~\ref{app:toe-constants-and-parameters}.
