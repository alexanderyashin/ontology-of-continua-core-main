\paragraph{Mathematics.}
The mathematics anchor is a deterministic replay lane rather than an empirical
benchmark family. It currently sits at verdict \occode{PASS} with replay state
\occode{PASS_REPLAYABLE}. No parameterized cases are required because the lane
tests strict closure ledgers and counterexample traces directly. Promotion
fails on the first replay divergence or on the first surviving counterexample.
Replay command:
\occode{validation/mathematics/replay.py}. The
lawful next action remains \occode{MAINTAIN_REPLAY_DISCIPLINE}.

\paragraph{Physics.}
The promoted physics packet is
\occode{OC13::PHYSICS::FOUNDATIONAL_CONSTANTS_AND_SPECTRA}, executed through
\occode{OC13::EXECUTION::PHYSICS::CONSTANTS_SPECTRA_TRANSPORT}. Held-out
residuals must remain within the declared tolerance band for every promoted
benchmark family. Any family outside tolerance, or any broken sign/order
constraint, falsifies the packet. Replay command:
\occode{validation/physics/replay.py}.
The current official replay basis is \occode{PARTIAL_OFFICIAL_PRIMARY_DATA},
with 30 covered cases, 20 held-out cases, mean absolute normalized error
0.188136 sigma, p95 0.64778 sigma, maximum 1.48 sigma, and zero tail breaches.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,nosep]
\item \occode{PHY_BENCH_001}: \occode{fine_structure_constant_residual} from \url{https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/index.html}; held-out policy: leave one family out for residual verification.
\item \occode{PHY_BENCH_002}: \occode{hydrogen_balmer_line_residual} from \url{https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/ASD/lines_form.html}; held-out policy: reserve transitions not used during packet fitting.
\item \occode{PHY_BENCH_003}: \occode{transport_scaling_residual} from \url{https://science.osti.gov/-/media/fes/pdf/workshop-reports/FES_Grand_Challenges_Report_final.pdf}; held-out policy: hold out one transport-regime family.
\end{itemize}
