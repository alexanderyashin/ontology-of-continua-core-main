\begin{abstract}
We introduce a bounded operational framework for event and fact stabilization under observation architectures. The framework is claim-ceiling controlled and does not replace quantum mechanics.
\end{abstract}
\section{Introduction}
The target problem is not metaphoric phenomenalization but a reproducible contract for when an event candidate may be treated as a stabilized fact.
\section{Related Work}
The paper is positioned against quantum instruments, POVMs, decoherence, quantum Darwinism, active inference, quantum-like cognition, and multi-agent consensus.
\section{Methods}
We define ObservationArchitecture, EventStabilizationContract, support classes, falsifiers, and a deterministic benchmark suite.
