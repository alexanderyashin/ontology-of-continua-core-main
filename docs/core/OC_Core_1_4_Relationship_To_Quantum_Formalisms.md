# OC Core 1.4 Relationship To Quantum Formalisms

Non-negotiable boundary:
1. OC Core 1.4 does not replace quantum mechanics.
2. A physical quantum claim requires Born-compatible statistics or explicit non-physical labeling.
3. Physics branch measurement architecture must be expressible through accepted objects: POVM, quantum instrument, channel, Lindblad/SME, or decoherence model.
4. Deterministic nonlinear updates are effective computational models unless accompanied by a separate no-signalling proof.
5. Public wording must label each claim as physical quantum theory, operational modeling, cognitive/AI analogy, or philosophical interpretation.
