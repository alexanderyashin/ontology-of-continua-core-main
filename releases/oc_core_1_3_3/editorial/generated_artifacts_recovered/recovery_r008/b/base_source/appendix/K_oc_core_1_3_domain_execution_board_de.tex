\paragraph{Mathematics.}
Validation remains bounded evidence recorded, and the lane is executable now because the
command path is purely deterministic. Caseset readiness is 0 parameterized out
of 0 cases with a minimum requirement of 31, while coverage and parameterization
remain not required. Promotion survives only if no replay divergence
and no surviving counterexample appear. The lawful command is
the public evidence replay route. The
required prerequisites remain the pinned source record, the benchmark manifest,
the numerical packet specification, the replay harness, and an explicit
falsifier. Blocking ids: none. No institute-run wave is required.

\paragraph{Physics.}
Validation remains bounded evidence recorded, and the lane stays in maintenance rather than
in expansion mode. Caseset readiness is 30 parameterized out of 30 cases, with
coverage \occode{PASS_CASESET_MINIMUM_REACHED} and parameterization
\occode{PREDICTIONS_DECLARED_FOR_ALL_CASES}. The acceptance criterion requires
held-out residuals to stay within the declared tolerance band for every
promoted benchmark family; any family outside tolerance or with broken
sign/order constraints falsifies the packet. The lawful command is
\occode{public evidence replay route --domain-id PHYSICS}. The reserve wave
Physics institute-run wave 1a remains on stand-by, not active.

\paragraph{Chemistry.}
Validation remains bounded evidence recorded, and the chemistry lane also sits in
maintenance mode. Caseset readiness is 30 parameterized out of 30 cases, with
coverage \occode{PASS_CASESET_MINIMUM_REACHED} and parameterization
\occode{PREDICTIONS_DECLARED_FOR_ALL_CASES}. The acceptance criterion requires
the declared chemistry benchmark families to remain within tolerance on
held-out compounds or reactions; a held-out thermochemical, spectral, or
kinetic family outside tolerance falsifies the packet. The lawful command is
\occode{public evidence replay route --domain-id CHEMISTRY}. The reserve wave
Chemistry institute-run wave 2a remains on stand-by.

\paragraph{Biology.}
Validation remains bounded evidence recorded, and the biological lane is executable now
only in maintenance mode. Caseset readiness is 30 parameterized out of 30
cases, with coverage \occode{PASS_CASESET_MINIMUM_REACHED} and parameterization
\occode{PREDICTIONS_DECLARED_FOR_ALL_CASES}. The acceptance criterion requires
the promoted biological packet to reproduce the declared measurable signatures
on held-out datasets without widening the claim boundary. If the bounded
signature family fails on the held-out datasets, the lane loses promotion. The
lawful command is
\occode{public evidence replay route --domain-id BIOLOGY}. The reserve wave
Biology institute-run wave 3a remains on stand-by.

\paragraph{Systems / Civilizational projection.}
Validation remains bounded evidence recorded, and the systems lane is likewise executable
only in maintenance mode. Caseset readiness is 30 parameterized out of 30
cases, with coverage \occode{PASS_CASESET_MINIMUM_REACHED} and parameterization
\occode{PREDICTIONS_DECLARED_FOR_ALL_CASES}. The acceptance criterion requires
the declared trajectory and regime-change observables to remain within
tolerance on held-out intervals; a missed turning point or out-of-band held-out
trajectory falsifies the packet. The lawful command is
\occode{public evidence replay route --domain-id SYSTEMS_CIVILIZATIONAL_PROJECTION}. The reserve wave
Systems institute-run wave 4a remains on stand-by.
