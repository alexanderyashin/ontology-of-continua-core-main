\label{sec:oc-core-1-3-technical-derivation-archive}

useful for formal audit but no longer belong in the flagship reading path of
the English master. The main body now carries the load-bearing narrative and
science-closure route; this archive preserves the compact module masters and
formal supplements needed to inspect the wider OC architecture without forcing
the reader through every per-level expansion in sequence.

The detailed directory-wide K-by-K expansions remain in the source tree as
source-native witnesses. What follows is the curated technical atlas that a
careful reader most often needs inside the monograph itself.
