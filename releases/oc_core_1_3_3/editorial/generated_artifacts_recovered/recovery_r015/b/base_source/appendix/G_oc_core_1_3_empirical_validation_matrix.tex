\section{Appendix H -- Empirical Evidence Boundary}
% R005_APPENDIX_READER_FRAME
Reader frame. This appendix supports the main manuscript by explaining corpus role, evidence class, audit route, and limitation boundary. It is not a detached raw dump.


\label{sec:oc-core-1-3-empirical-evidence-boundary-appendix}

This appendix summarizes the empirical evidence boundary for the public monograph. Executed retrospective replay and replay-QA rows are cited in the methods companion and distributed in the public evidence package. Planned or pending rows are not promoted as bounded replay evidence.


A promoted empirical row must carry formula, source snapshot, split or retrospective replay role, observed value, residual or uncertainty, comparator, negative control, falsifier, and replay hash. Rows without that complete stack remain protocol-ready or background research.
