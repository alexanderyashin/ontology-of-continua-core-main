# Checklist

- [x] Double-blind reviewing procedure; identifying information must be removed from the blinded manuscript.
- [x] Separate title page with author details, acknowledgements, disclosures, and funding information.
- [x] Abstract of 150 to 250 words, 4 to 6 keywords, JEL codes where relevant, and APA-style references.
- [x] Decimal headings with no more than three levels.
- [x] Tables, artwork, SI, data availability, ethical standards, and competing interests are required.
