# Public Decision Memo: K0 Structural-Realist Extension

Status: `OWNER_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

Outbound: `NO_SEND`.

## Verdict

Keep this work as v1.3.2 support/frontier appendix material. Do not patch K0 and
do not add promoted claims to the canonical claim ledger.

## What Survived

- Distinction language survives as a non-core interpretation of bounded
  distinguishability.
- `Theta0` survives as the primitive operational boundary; the research does not
  eliminate it.
- Contextual/topos language survives as an optional local-admissibility and
  gluing-obstruction note.
- Symmetry/gauge axis generation is the strongest branch, but only at a
  toy-model representation level.
- Quantum-gravity discussion survives only as bounded comparison vocabulary.

## What Failed

- Rewriting K0 as a dynamic distinction calculus.
- Recasting K0 as a topos, gauge theory, or physics foundation.
- Treating toy models as physical confirmation.
- Making unbounded QG, QFT/GR, or all-physics derivation claims.

## Integration Decision

Add this packet to the v1.3.2 release candidate as reviewer-support material.
The packet is not a release permission, not an external publication event, and
not a core upgrade. Owner approval remains required before any outbound release.
