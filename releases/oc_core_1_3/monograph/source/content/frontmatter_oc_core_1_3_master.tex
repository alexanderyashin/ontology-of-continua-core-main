\begin{abstract}
Ontology of Continua (OC) Core~1.3.2 is the current release of the Core~1.3
line and a single reference volume for a theory
of continua: structured objects that arise, persist, collapse, leave residue,
and can later be classified as new residue-supported live continua under the
rebirth predicate. The problem it addresses is practical as well as formal: how
to compare such structures across domains without turning shared vocabulary
into an unrestricted predictive claim.

The monograph contributes a bounded public account of that theory. It teaches
the formal core, records the defended proof route, reports the empirical
results that meet the stated evidence criteria, and states what can now be
used in practice. Its science/SPOT \occode{PASS} verdict is scoped to
\occode{CORE_1_3_SCIENCE_ONLY}; it keeps theorem-native claims, bounded
empirical claims, and practical or comparative claims visibly separate.

The limit is explicit. This volume does not claim unrestricted prediction or
replacement of local scientific models. Its main body presents the defended
theory, while its appendices preserve the evidence record, benchmark
apparatus, synthesis support materials, and practical-comparison archive.
\end{abstract}
