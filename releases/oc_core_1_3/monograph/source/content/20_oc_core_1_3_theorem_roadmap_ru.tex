\section{Theorem Roadmap, Proof Navigation, and Reader Entry Paths}

One persistent weakness of large foundational manuscripts is that they force every reader to
discover the proof architecture by brute force. That is acceptable for an internal draft but not

Core 1.3 therefore adds an explicit теорему roadmap inside the master volume itself. The purpose

\subsection{Three lawful entry paths into the same scientific object}

The monograph is one scientific object, but it should not pretend that every serious reader
enters it in the same order. The публичныйation architecture is easier to trust when those entry
paths are stated explicitly.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Editorial route.}
      A journal editor or hostile reviewer should be able to inspect the abstract, the present
      теорему roadmap, the main теорему chain in Section~\ref{sec:results}, the collapse and
      rebirth material, the 1.3 source-audit layer, and the reviewer-navigation appendix. This
      route is designed to answer the first two hard questions quickly: what exactly is being
      defended, and where does the support boundary stop?
\item \textbf{Formal route.}
      A reader interested primarily in correctness should move through the axioms, the operator
      shell, the теорему-bearing chapters, the K-level doctrine, the appendices, and the explicit
      1.3 consistency dossier. This route keeps the dependency chain visible and makes it harder
      for explanatory prose to masquerade as proof.
\item \textbf{Didactic route.}
      A scientifically serious non-specialist should be able to read the introduction, background,
      model, present roadmap, the worked examples in
      Section~\ref{sec:oc13-worked-examples}, and then return to the denser теорему-bearing
      chapters with a more stable map in hand. This route is slower, but it is honest: it does
      not ask the reader to infer the whole architecture from compressed теорему names alone.
\end{enumerate}

The existence of these routes is not editorial indulgence. It is part of scientific rigor. A
foundational manuscript that cannot be entered cleanly by multiple serious readers becomes easier
to misread and easier to oversell.

\subsection{Why the master volume must remain larger than the defended статья}

The bounded journal-core claim is not the whole scientific content of the monograph. It is the
most defensible публичный теорему route at the present release boundary. The master volume remains
larger because it carries at least five scientific burdens that the short работу alone should not
be forced to bear.

\begin{enumerate}
\item It preserves the inherited axiomatic and operator shell in one readable object.
\item It carries the full K-level doctrine and explains why the hierarchy is fixed as
      \(K_0,\dots,K_{12}\) rather than cut short by editorial convenience.
\item It keeps the broader domain-facing modules, falsifiability routes, and projection
      boundaries available for scrutiny instead of hiding them behind internal registries.
\item It holds the 1.3 source-audit, chronology, and consistency apparatus inside the scientific
      object itself.
\item It teaches the reader how later artifacts are derived so that journal-core, outreach, and
      owner-facing channels do not become separate interpretive universes.
\end{enumerate}

In other words, the master monograph is not larger than the статья because the release lacks
discipline. It is larger because a serious foundational framework needs one place where its
vocabulary, proof routes, reader guidance, figures, appendices, and limitations still coexist.

\subsection{The shortest hostile-review checklist}

If a reviewer wishes to challenge the release at its most vulnerable points, the logically
efficient order is the following.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Check whether collapse is tied to the loss of admissible realization rather than to loose
      metaphor.
\item Check whether the irreversibility rule really forbids restoration of the original
      continuum as numerically the same entity.
\item Check whether residue and rebirth are used as controlled classifications rather than as
      decorative narrative labels.
\item Check whether any excluded lift-heavy теорему family is smuggled back into the positive
      ограниченного аргумента.
\item Check whether the journal-core extraction says anything stronger than the master monograph.
\item Check whether the K-level doctrine is structurally justified or merely counted upward by
      enthusiasm.
\end{enumerate}

If the manuscript survives those checks, it deserves a slower and fuller reading. If it fails
them, no amount of later prose can repair the result. Making that sequence explicit is part of
the 1.3 improvement: the monograph now teaches the reviewer where the real risk lies instead of
burying it inside a vast undifferentiated shell.
