\section{Practical Consequences, Predictive Power, and Use of OC Core~1.3}
\label{sec:oc-core-1-3-practical-utility}

OC Core~1.3 is practically useful for a bounded set of actions: lawful packet
selection, bounded residual prediction, anomaly screening, state-transition
auditing, regime-shift monitoring, and cross-domain route selection. This
chapter states what the framework lets the reader predict, audit, decide,
compare, and use within those explicit bounds and under clearly stated support
classes. It does not present OC as an unrestricted universal prediction
system.

The practical value lies in step-by-step playbooks and a comparison matrix.
That matrix keeps three questions separate. First, it records what prior science
already does well inside its native lanes. Second, it states where OC adds
bounded bridges for route selection, falsifier testing, and replay discipline. Third,
it marks where a local model remains superior in its own lane.

This practical framing is comparative rather than replacement-oriented.
Specialised local model families in physics, chemistry, biology, and the
Systems / Civilizational projection lane remain strong inside their native
lanes. OC's present claim is narrower: it aligns selected bounded packets
through shared route selection, falsifier tests, and replay checks, while each
domain-level closure remains local to its validated theorem record and
validated replay record.
Appendix~\ref{sec:oc-core-1-3-practical-utility-atlas} gives the row-level
trace anchors for that operational claim. The surrounding comparison
vocabulary is contextualised by established model families
\parencite{goldenfeld_lectures,wilson_renormalization,kauffman_autocatalytic,raf_networks,hebb_organization,friston_free_energy}. The
canonical utility atlas also carries theorem-native mathematics and one
cross-domain unified-science row; the opening paragraphs foreground the
empirical lanes because they provide the most immediate replay-tested use cases
for external readers.

This chapter draws from the canonical practical-utility atlas so that
reader-facing statements about usefulness stay tied to the closed theorem
record, closed replay record, and closed falsifier record. The Practical Utility and Model
Comparison Atlas (Appendix~\ref{sec:oc-core-1-3-practical-utility-atlas})
carries the detailed support surface. It contains the full use-case table, the
operational playbook table, the same-claim baseline matrix, the serious model
comparison matrix, the fragmentation-to-closure map, the readiness and
open-frontier matrix, and the trace-anchor matrix in one place.
