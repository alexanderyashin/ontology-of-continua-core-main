\section{Institute-Run Measurement and Observation Program}
\label{sec:oc-core-1-3-institute-run-measurement-program}

This appendix records the stand-by measurement waves that would become mandatory
only if pinned official or open sources stopped closing the declared empirical
lanes honestly. At the present release boundary, all four empirical domains
remain on official- or open-data routes; the institute-run waves are retained
as controlled fallback mechanisms rather than as active evidence channels.

\paragraph{Interpretive rule.}
These waves are not alternate proof routes. They are reserve observation
programs that become lawful only after the official or open route fails to
cover the declared observable family. Until such a trigger is reached, the
current release remains committed to the pinned replay discipline already
documented in the empirical protocol chapters and benchmark appendices.
