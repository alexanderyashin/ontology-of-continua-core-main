\subsection{K-Level Parameter and Numerical Tables}
\label{app:toe-constants-and-parameters}

This subsection collects the numerical and parameter tables that support the
reader-facing K-level apparatus of the flagship volume. It sits immediately
after the general K-level tables because it provides the concrete structural,
empirical, and derived quantities referenced later by the unified synthesis and the
practical chapter.

Values are given together with uncertainties or admissible ranges when
applicable. All constants are quoted in SI unless stated otherwise. Where the
continuum formalism introduces structural parameters not directly measured, the
table records the corresponding functional form, numerical estimate, or bounded
range rather than leaving the row implicit.
