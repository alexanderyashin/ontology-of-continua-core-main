# OC Core 1.3.1 Corporate Projection Atlas

Status: PASS

Publication mode: tracked supplementary governance, not a v1.3.1 public release
asset.

Canonical public record: https://zenodo.org/records/19741958

## Guardrail

No product or business claim may outrun the canonical science and release gates.
Product rows are product surfaces and requirements, not shipped software claims.
Business rows are governance and diligence uses, not financial advice, market
sizing, revenue forecasts, or institutional endorsement.

## Projection Classes

| Class | Rows | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| INSTRUMENT | 3 | Closure audit, theorem-to-observable routing, replay/falsifier use. |
| TOOLING | 3 | Packet selection, evidence checklist, publication/release gate checking. |
| PRODUCT | 4 | Product surfaces that can be scoped from the released evidence base. |
| BUSINESS | 4 | Bounded corporate governance and diligence use cases. |
| PUBLICATION | 7 | Published article-family and reviewer-route surfaces. |

## Instrument

- `CORP::INSTRUMENT::CLOSURE_AUDITOR`: uses the practical utility atlas,
  release inventory, and public release gate certificate to classify closure,
  missing evidence, and demotion or promotion.
- `CORP::INSTRUMENT::THEOREM_TO_OBSERVABLE_ROUTER`: routes bounded problem
  classes to observable families and packet boundaries.
- `CORP::INSTRUMENT::REPLAY_FALSIFIER_INSTRUMENT`: uses the simulation ledger,
  simulation guide, and dataset manifest for replay and falsifier discipline.

## Tooling

- `CORP::TOOLING::PACKET_SELECTOR`: selects lawful packets and frontier
  fallbacks from the practical atlas and domain packet set.
- `CORP::TOOLING::EVIDENCE_CHECKLIST`: turns claims, refs, and reproducibility
  requirements into reviewer-ready checklists.
- `CORP::TOOLING::PUBLICATION_RELEASE_GATE_CHECKER`: keeps release checks
  read-only for this supplementary pack and distinct from public release
  mutation.

## Product

- `CORP::PRODUCT::SCIENTIFIC_CLOSURE_CONSOLE`: closure dashboard specification.
- `CORP::PRODUCT::DOMAIN_PACKET_WORKBENCH`: domain packet workflow surface.
- `CORP::PRODUCT::REVIEWER_ROUTE_BUILDER`: reviewer route and claim-to-evidence
  product surface.
- `CORP::PRODUCT::RELEASE_EVIDENCE_PACKAGER`: evidence packaging and DOI
  integrity surface.

## Business

- `CORP::BUSINESS::R_AND_D_GOVERNANCE`: governance lanes for research portfolios.
- `CORP::BUSINESS::SCIENTIFIC_DUE_DILIGENCE`: bounded scientific diligence
  checklist.
- `CORP::BUSINESS::PORTFOLIO_ROADMAP_TRIAGE`: evidence-based roadmap triage.
- `CORP::BUSINESS::INSTITUTIONAL_REVIEW_SUPPORT`: institutional review support
  without endorsement claims.
