\section{Corporate Science Projection Pack}
\label{sec:oc-core-1-3-1-corporate-projection-pack}

This appendix source records a supplementary corporate projection layer for
OC Core~1.3.1. It is tracked governance material, not a new public release
asset. The canonical public record remains the Zenodo new-version record
\occode{10.5281/zenodo.19741958} under concept DOI
\occode{10.5281/zenodo.17899134}.

\paragraph{Rule.}
No product or business projection may outrun the canonical science and release
gates. The rows below are usable only as bounded instrument, tooling, product,
business, or publication surfaces. They are not revenue forecasts, investment
advice, institutional endorsement, or a claim that unreleased software already
exists.

\paragraph{Projection classes.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em,nosep]
  \item \occode{INSTRUMENT}: closure auditor, theorem-to-observable router,
        and replay/falsifier instrument.
  \item \occode{TOOLING}: packet selector, evidence checklist, and
        publication/release gate checker.
  \item \occode{PRODUCT}: Scientific Closure Console, Domain Packet Workbench,
        Reviewer Route Builder, and Release Evidence Packager.
  \item \occode{BUSINESS}: R\&D governance, scientific due diligence,
        portfolio/roadmap triage, and institutional review support.
  \item \occode{PUBLICATION}: master monograph, journal core,
        methods/simulation companion, critique closure companion, readable
        overview, unignorable spine, and reviewer packet.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Validation.}
The governing surface is
\occode{releases/oc_core_1_3_1/editorial/corporate_projections/OC_CORE_1_3_1_CORPORATE_PROJECTION_ATLAS_latest.json}.
The local validator is
\occode{tools/validate_oc_corporate_projection_pack_v1.py}. It checks that all
five projection classes are present, that usable rows trace to existing
repository sources, that product and business rows use only bounded support
classes, that publication rows point to existing OC Core~1.3.1 release PDFs or
release audits, and that the canonical/superseded Zenodo metadata remains
consistent.
