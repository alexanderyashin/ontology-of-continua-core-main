\section{Institute Run Measurement Program}
This appendix records the measurement-program route used by the public monograph baseline. Its job is to keep empirical execution, replay, benchmark review, and escalation policy inspectable without inserting operational metadata into the main argument.
\begin{longtable}{L{0.28\textwidth}L{0.62\textwidth}}
\toprule
Layer & Review function\\
\midrule
Benchmark definition & States the measurable object before execution begins.\\
Replay transcript & Records whether the execution can be repeated under the declared inputs.\\
Comparator pressure & Prevents a local measurement from being promoted beyond its comparator class.\\
Escalation rule & Sends unresolved measurement failures back to the scientific graph rather than to prose padding.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
