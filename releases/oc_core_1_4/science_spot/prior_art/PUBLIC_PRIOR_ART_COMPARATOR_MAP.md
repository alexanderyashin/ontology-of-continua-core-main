# Public Prior-Art Comparator Map

This map records nearby public comparator families used to bound OC Core 1.4 claims.
It is not a priority claim and not evidence that OC is accepted by those traditions.

- `PRIOR-AUTOPOIESIS`: Autopoiesis and biological autonomy - Does not prove OC; constrains biological projection language.
- `PRIOR-EFFECTIVE-COMPLEXITY`: Effective complexity - Does not supply a universal OC metric.
- `PRIOR-PREDICTABILITY`: Predictability and information in measured systems - Prediction is a projection route, not the whole OC ontology.
- `PRIOR-IIT`: Integrated information - OC does not import consciousness-specific authority.
- `PRIOR-SCALING-RG`: Scaling and renormalization group - OC borrows caution about declared transformations, not proof authority.
- `PRIOR-PERCOLATION`: Percolation and connectivity thresholds - Percolation rigor remains model-local.
