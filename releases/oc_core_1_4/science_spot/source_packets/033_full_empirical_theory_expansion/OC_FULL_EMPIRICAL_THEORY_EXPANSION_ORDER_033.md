# OC 033 Full Empirical Theory Expansion Order

## Authority

033 extends 032 from current-source closure to a full empirical expansion queue.
It must not claim that OC is a full empirical theory of all domains and
crown-stone consequences while any source-defined or discovered row remains
`REQUIRES_NEW_LOCAL_DATA`.

032 is the baseline for current evidence ceilings. 033 adds the wider
monograph/project domain projection surface, crown-stone inventory checks, and
reviewer empirical attack/response obligations.

obligation with a named next evidence packet. Any such row forces
`full_empirical_closeout_allowed = 0`.

## Scientific Rules

- Domain claims need local observables, constraints, operators, invariants,
  falsifier, evidence refs, replay/replication, no blockers, and claim ceiling.
- Mathematics can be formally/descriptively proved, but not called empirical
  unless a future empirical package exists.
- Named crown stones need exact source statement, closed theorem chain, no
  refuted dependency, falsifier, and local evidence if empirical.
- Core vocabulary alone never proves a crown stone.
