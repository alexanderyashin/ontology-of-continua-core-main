# OC 037 Article Boundary

## Allowed

- K2 Biology has bounded empirical support for the BIOLOGY benchmark surface:
  biological state transitions and response signatures.
- K2 Biology remains formally proved as a disciplined projection chapter where
  it says OC does not replace biology and requires observable biological
  variables.
- K2 emergence and descriptive-dimension wording is allowed as a criterion for
  measurement, prediction, intervention, or explanation, not as universal
  biological proof.

## Forbidden

- Do not say K2 Biology is fully empirically proved.
- Do not say BIOLOGY benchmark replay proves wound healing, biofilm persistence,
  fever, action potentials, evolution, homeostasis, or all biological emergence.
- Do not promote K2 from coarse BIOLOGY `PASS` alone.
- Do not say the whole OC domain/crown program has full empirical closeout.

## Promotion Rule

K2 may receive full chapter-level empirical promotion only when every K2
subclaim is either empirically proved with matching local evidence, formally
closed as nonempirical, or rejected/repaired with counterevidence, and no K2
follow-up requirement remains open.
