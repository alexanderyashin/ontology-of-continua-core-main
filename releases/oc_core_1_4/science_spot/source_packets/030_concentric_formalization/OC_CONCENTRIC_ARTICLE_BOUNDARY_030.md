# OC Concentric Article Boundary 030

## Allowed

The article may say:

- OC-M has a formal minimal metaontology center;
- OC-030 extends that center outward by contour: center, adequacy, operators,
  K-ladder, theorem schemas, laws, crown boundary, and domain projection
  boundary;
- K0-K12 is a reference depth-12 instance of a parametric K-ladder;
- six continuum operators are minimal under typed semantic effect signatures;
- domain projections require local observables, constraints, operators,
  invariants, falsifiers, and evidence bindings.

## Forbidden

The article must not say:

- every real conflict births a dimension or collapses;
- K0-K12 is an absolute unique cosmic count;
- the six operators are globally minimal outside typed semantic effects;
- laws are article-eligible without falsifiers;
- crown stones are proved by the core alone;
- domain projections are proved by source vocabulary or replay labels alone.

## Promotion Rule

Only rows with closed dependencies and statuses
`PROVED_WITH_FORMAL_PROOF`, `REFUTED_AND_REPAIRED_WITH_PROOF`, or
`DOMAIN_PROJECTION_PROVED_WITH_LOCAL_EVIDENCE` may produce positive article
claims. `DOMAIN_INSTANTIATION_REQUIRED_BY_THEOREM` may produce only boundary
language.
