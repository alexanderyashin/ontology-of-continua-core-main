# OC 036 Article Boundary

## Allowed

- K1 Chemistry has bounded empirical support for the chemistry benchmark
  surface: thermochemistry, spectra, and kinetics/equilibrium envelopes.
- K1 Chemistry remains formally proved as a disciplined projection chapter.
- K1 formose/RAF and origin-of-life-style organization remain open until a
  local chapter-level evidence packet is built.

## Forbidden

- Do not say K1 Chemistry is fully empirically proved.
- Do not say existing NIST/DOE chemistry benchmark replay proves formose,
  RAF closure, prebiotic persistence, or origin of life.
- Do not say the whole OC domain/crown program has full empirical closeout.

## Promotion Rule

K1 may receive full chapter-level empirical promotion only when every K1
subclaim is either empirically proved with matching local evidence, formally
closed as nonempirical, or rejected/repaired with counterevidence, and no
K1 follow-up requirement remains open.
