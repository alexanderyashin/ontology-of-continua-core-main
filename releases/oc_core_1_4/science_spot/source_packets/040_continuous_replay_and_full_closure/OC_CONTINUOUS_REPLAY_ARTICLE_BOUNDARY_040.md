# OC 040 Article Boundary

## Allowed

- OC may say that a K/domain/crown row is empirically proved only at the exact claim
  ceiling recorded in the 040 DB and only when the replay/proof artifacts are present.
- OC may say that a row remains in active acquisition when no lawful local/public
  dataset has yet closed its evidence packet.
- OC may use formal projection wording for nonempirical rows when the proof chain is
  replayed and the article gate marks the row nonempirical.

## Forbidden

- Do not say that all K-level domain projections are empirically proved while any 040
  row remains in `ACTIVE_DATA_ACQUISITION_REQUIRED`, `REPLAY_IN_PROGRESS`, or
  `PROOF_IN_PROGRESS`.
- Do not call Natural Stories evidence for roadside perception.
- Do not call wound-only GEO transcriptomic evidence a complete wound/biofilm/homeostasis
  packet unless biofilm and homeostasis evidence are also present.
- Do not promote crown stones from OC vocabulary alone; they require named theorem
  chains and, when empirical, local evidence.

## Closeout Rule

Full empirical closeout is forbidden unless the 040 validator reports zero open rows,
zero overpromoted rows, and closed dependency chains for every promoted row.
