# OC Article Integration Ledger 028

## Allowed Article Thesis

OC-U may be described as a universal metamodel for articulated descriptions of
system dynamics. Its universality is formal-descriptive: when a domain supplies
states, observables, constraints, operators, projections, invariants, repair
classes, extension classes, and evidence bindings, OC-U gives a total taxonomy
for structured pressure in that declared description.

## Forbidden As Promoted Wording

The article must not promote the following as true claims:

| Forbidden wording | Correct replacement |
| --- | --- |
| Every real-world conflict produces dimension birth or collapse. | Structured pressure has four OC-U outcomes: absorption, extension, collapse, or underdetermination. |
| Lean proves OC true of the world. | Lean proves the OC-U theorem chain under declared definitions. |
| TOE gates already proved universal OC. | TOE gates found remaining gaps; 027/028 repair the metamodel claim and preserve domain obligations. |
| The six operators are globally minimal and unique. | The six operator roles are separated under role-preserving effect signatures. |
| K0-K12 is the unique cosmic hierarchy. | K0-K12 is a reference stratification, not a unique partition of reality. |
| Domain projections are validated by the OC core alone. | Domain projections need local observables, constraints, operators, falsifiers, and evidence bindings. |
| Crown stones are established by the core alone. | Crown stones need their own theorem chains or domain evidence. |

## Publication Boundary

Article-eligible wording can now say:

OC-U is a universal metamodel for articulated descriptions of system dynamics,
with a formally checked stress-response taxonomy and a non-inflation rule for
domain projections.

Article-eligible wording cannot say:

OC-U proves every concrete domain claim, every crown stone, or every external
scientific projection before local instantiation evidence is supplied.
