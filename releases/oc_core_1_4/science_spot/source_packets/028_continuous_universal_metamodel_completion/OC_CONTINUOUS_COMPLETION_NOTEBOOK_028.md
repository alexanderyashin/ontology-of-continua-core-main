## Scientific Interpretation

The repaired universal claim is strong but precise:

OC-U is a universal language for describable system dynamics because any
articulated description can be typed into the OC-U primitive package and its
structured pressure can be classified by the total stress-response taxonomy.

The repaired claim is not:

- a proof that every real-world conflict causes new dimension or collapse;
- a proof that K0-K12 is the unique hierarchy of reality;
- a proof that the six operator names are globally minimal;
- a proof that domain projections are validated without local evidence;
- a proof that crown stones are already established.
