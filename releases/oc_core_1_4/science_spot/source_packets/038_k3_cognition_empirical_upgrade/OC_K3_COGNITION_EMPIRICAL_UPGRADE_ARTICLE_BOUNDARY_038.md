# OC 038 Article Boundary

## Allowed

- K3 Cognition may be described as a formally bounded projection discipline.
- K3 may say that cognitive evidence must bind to behavior, error patterns,
  reaction time, report, task switching, learning curves, neural recordings,
  developmental change, or overload breakdown.
- K3 may distinguish ordinary revision from breakdown as a protocol that must
  be tested locally.

## Forbidden

- Do not say K3 Cognition is empirically proved.
- Do not say crowded-room ambiguity or roadside perception is proved without
  local reaction-time/error/response or perception/action data.
- Do not promote K3 from analogy, prose examples, or absent `COGNITION` matrix
  rows.
- Do not say the whole OC domain/crown program has full empirical closeout.

## Promotion Rule

K3 may receive full empirical promotion only when every K3 empirical subclaim
has a matching local dataset, replay script, acceptance thresholds, and
independent verification, while no K3 follow-up requirement remains open.
