# OC Empirical Crown And Domain Article Boundary 032

## Allowed

The article may say:

- OC has an empirical/crown closure ledger for the current source-defined
  domains and crown-boundary surface;
- physics, chemistry, biology, and systems/civilizational projection are
  empirically proved with evidence only at their recorded validated-predictive
  no-send ceiling;
- mathematics is proved as a descriptive/proof-expansion projection, not as an
  empirical prediction;
- crown-stone promotion is proved only as a conditional corollary rule requiring
  dependency closure and local evidence.

## Forbidden

The article must not say:

- all possible domains are empirically proved;
- mathematics has an empirical prediction package;
- crown stones are proved by the core vocabulary alone;
- any domain row exceeds its `claim_level` and `claim_ceiling`;
- public, clinical, financial, physics, biology, or civilizational claims are
  release-ready beyond the local no-send/evidence ceiling.

## Promotion Rule

Only rows with one of the 032 allowed final statuses may be cited. Positive
empirical wording is allowed only for `EMPIRICALLY_PROVED_WITH_EVIDENCE` rows.
`PROVED_AS_DESCRIPTIVE_PROJECTION` rows may produce descriptive wording only.
`PROVED_AS_CONDITIONAL_CROWN_COROLLARY` rows may state only the conditional
corollary rule, not a named crown-stone result. Rejected rows may be cited only
as guard/counterevidence.
