# OC 035 Article Boundary

## Allowed

- OC has formal projection packets for K-domain chapters where the source
  provides a disciplined translation, falsifier, and non-replacement boundary.
- OC has coarse empirical domain evidence only at the exact ceilings already
  established in 031 and 032.
- Named Crown Stones are inventoried from the Crown Stone registry, but remain
  article-ineligible as proved crown-stones until theorem chains and evidence
  packages close per stone.

## Forbidden

- Do not say that all K-domain chapters are empirically proved.
- Do not say that the K1-K12 projection chapters are new empirical domain
  science.
- Do not say that Crown Stones are proved by core vocabulary alone.
- Do not say that OC is a fully proved empirical theory of all domains and
  crown-stone consequences while the 035 DB reports empirical upgrade
  obligations.

## Promotion Rule

`FULL_EMPIRICAL_CLOSEOUT` is allowed only when:

- every packet row is terminal;
- every empirical upgrade requirement is closed;
- every crown stone has a named statement, theorem chain, falsifier, and local
  evidence if empirical;
- every empirical attack-response source unit is mapped to evidence,
  counterevidence, or a repaired claim.
