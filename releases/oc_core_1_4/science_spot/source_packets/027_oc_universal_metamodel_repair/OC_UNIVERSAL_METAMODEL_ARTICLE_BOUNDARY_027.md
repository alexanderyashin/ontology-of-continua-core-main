# OC Universal Metamodel Article Boundary 027

## Allowed Thesis

OC-U is a universal metamodel for articulated descriptions of system dynamics.
It supplies a formal taxonomy for how structured pressure in a declared
description can be handled:

- absorption by existing operator;
- extension, refinement, or new axis;
- collapse of a declared invariant;
- underdetermined/out-of-scope until observables and evidence are supplied.

## Forbidden Thesis

The article must not claim:

- every real-world conflict produces dimension birth or collapse;
- Lean proves OC true of the world without domain instantiation;
- TOE gates already proved the universal metamodel;
- domain projection is validated by vocabulary resemblance alone;
- K0-K12 is the unique cosmic hierarchy.

## Correct Universal Claim

The universality is metamodel universality. Any candidate system-description
can be placed into OC-U if it declares states, observables, constraints,
operators, invariants, projections, repair/extension classes, and evidence
bindings. Whether a real domain satisfies those declarations is a separate
domain-instantiation question.
