# OC Universal Metamodel Root-Cause Audit 027

## What Went Wrong

The earlier proof stack did not prove OC as a universal metamodel of reality.
It proved weaker or different surfaces:

- extraction of formalism from source material;
- simulation and proof-trace gates for local theorem-like rows;
- proof-obligation closure ratios;
- governance readiness or closure surfaces;
- conditional formal claims under declared definitions.

The Lean formalization route explicitly states that Lean proves conditional
consequence from stated definitions and assumptions. It does not prove that
the world instantiates those definitions.

## Why 025 Refuted The Old Central Claim

The old two-outcome reading said every contradiction causes dimension birth or
collapse. A system with an existing repair operator is a counterexample: the
conflict is absorbed without event-level new axis and without collapse.

Therefore 025 was right about the old wording.

## Where 026 Was Insufficient

026 repaired OC into a bounded formal theory. That was honest but did not meet
the owner goal of a universal metamodel.

## Correct Repair

The repair is not to force the old fork back into the core. The repair is to
expand the taxonomy so that existing repair/absorption and underdetermination
are first-class OC outcomes. Then OC-U can be universal over articulated
descriptions while remaining honest about domain instantiation.
