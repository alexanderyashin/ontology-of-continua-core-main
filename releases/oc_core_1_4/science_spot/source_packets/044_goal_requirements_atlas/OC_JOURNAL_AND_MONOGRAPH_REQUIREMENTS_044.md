# OC 044 Journal And Monograph Requirements

Journal and book readiness are separated. A proof ledger is necessary but not sufficient; reviewer-facing and reader-facing artifacts must pass too.

| id | lane | requirement | status | acceptance criterion | blocker |

| SCI-003 | SCIENTIFIC | Operator, K, domain, and crown proof graph | SATISFIED_WITH_ARTIFACTS | Internal contours and crown/domain closures have DB-backed proof, counterproof, or repaired-proof rows. | none |
