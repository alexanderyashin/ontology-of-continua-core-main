# OC Theory Conversion Order 024

This is the controlling order for converting OC Core 1.4 from a central
hypothesis plus framework into an explicit bounded theory with proofs.

## Objective

Build a journal-safe OC theory core. The target result is not a universal
empirical law over every domain. The target result is an axiomatized/formal
theory with:

- stated primitives;
- explicit axioms or theorem derivations;
- nontrivial proved consequences;
- countermodel and falsifier discipline;
- a claim boundary that prevents unsupported crown-stone, domain, release,
  ROI, legal, or universal claims.

## Scope Split

Internal OC theory claims are the foundation:

- primitive objects;
- central principle;
- operator catalog and minimality;
- K-level hierarchy;
- theorem and law candidates;
- crown stones;
- domain projections.

External mathematical open problems are application/frontier tests. They do
not decide whether OC itself can become a theory. Existing 023 work on
`OC14-N001` is preserved as frontier evidence, but it is not the foundation
route for OC theory conversion.

## Theory Status Rules

Allowed terminal statuses for internal theory claims:

- `PROVED_THEOREM`;
- `AXIOM_ACCEPTED_WITH_BOUNDARY`;
- `REFUTED_AND_REPAIRED`;
- `EXCLUDED_FROM_CORE_AS_CONJECTURE`.

Article eligibility is stricter:

- article-eligible claims must be `PROVED_THEOREM` or
  `AXIOM_ACCEPTED_WITH_BOUNDARY`;
- all dependencies must be closed;
- proof body or axiom boundary must be present;
- verification note must be present;
- forbidden wording must be listed.

No claim may be article-eligible because it was demoted, hidden, made
supporting-only, or removed from publication.

## Research Order

1. Primitive objects:
   description, system, state, axis, continuum, boundary, operator,
   constraint, contradiction, resolution, collapse, dimension birth.
2. Central principle:
   decide theorem from stricter primitives, axiom/postulate with boundary, or
   false/too broad claim requiring repair.
3. Operators:
   recover source operator list, define type signatures, prove local closure
   and composition rules, then prove local minimality through independence
   countermodels.
4. K-level hierarchy:
   recover K0-K12 definitions, prove construction rule, distinctness
   invariants, non-collapse between levels, and count/partition theorem.
   Unproved high levels become conjectural extensions.
5. Theorem and law candidates:
   convert each theorem-like sentence into a formal proposition with
   assumptions, conclusion, falsifier, proof or refutation, and dependency
   closure.
6. Crown stones and domain projections:
   allow only as proved consequences or explicitly labelled conjectural
   applications.

## Non-Negotiable Boundary

The sentence:

`An unresolved contradiction either gives rise to a new dimension of
description or collapses the configuration that cannot resolve it.`

is not to be called proved unless it is derived from stricter primitives.
If it is independent but useful, it becomes an axiom/postulate. If it is false
or too broad, repair it and mark the older wording as refuted or excluded.

## Closeout Guard

The program is not complete until:

- every internal core claim has a terminal status;
- the central principle has a theorem/axiom/refutation decision;
- at least one nontrivial theorem chain is closed;
- the article boundary has no open-dependency promoted claims.

Any short progress update before then is a handoff, not completion.
