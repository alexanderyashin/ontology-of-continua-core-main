# OC Theory Article Boundary 024

This file controls journal-safe wording while the 024 theory conversion is in
progress.

## Current Allowed Framing

Allowed:

- OC Core is an axiomatization candidate and formal research framework for
  continua, boundaries, operators, contradiction, dimension birth, and
  collapse.
- OC currently has a central hypothesis under conversion into theorem,
  axiom, or repaired narrower principle.
- OC can submit a bounded article once the primitive package and at least one
  nontrivial theorem chain are closed.

Forbidden for now:

- OC has proved a universal law across all domains.
- The central principle is proved.
- The operator set is minimal.
- K0-K12 is the only possible hierarchy.
- Crown stones are established consequences.
- Domain projections validate the theory globally.
- External open problems are solved by OC unless independently proved.

## Central Principle Boundary

`An unresolved contradiction either gives rise to a new dimension of
description or collapses the configuration that cannot resolve it.`

Current article-safe wording:

`OC studies a proposed principle: under stated trigger conditions, an
unresolved incompatibility may force either a necessary new descriptive axis or
loss of the prior configuration. The 024 program decides whether this principle
is a theorem, an axiom, or a narrower repaired claim.`

Updated 024 status:

`OC-THEORY-CP-001` is accepted as `AXIOM_ACCEPTED_WITH_BOUNDARY`, not as a
proved universal theorem.

Current article-safe wording after 024-CP001:

`OC is an axiomatized framework built around a bounded central schema: when
active constraints cannot be jointly satisfied by admissible same-description
repair while preserving a target organization predicate, OC classifies the
core continuation as either necessary descriptive extension or loss of that
organization predicate.`

Allowed stronger wording only after DB closure:

  `PROVED_THEOREM`;
- `central principle is an axiom/postulate` only if `OC-THEORY-CP-001` is
  `AXIOM_ACCEPTED_WITH_BOUNDARY`;
- `central principle repaired` only if `OC-THEORY-CP-001` is
  `REFUTED_AND_REPAIRED` with replacement statement.

## Article Acceptance Gate

The first journal article may be prepared when:

- primitives are closed;
- central principle status is decided;
- one nontrivial theorem chain is closed;
- operator and K-level claims are either proved, axiomatized with boundary, or
  explicitly excluded from the first article;
- every promoted claim has a proof or axiom boundary row in the DB.

OC claim invoking the central schema must name description, system,
constraints, organization predicate, admissible repair class, unresolvedness
argument, and either necessary new axis or collapse certificate.

schema.`

collapse across all domains.`
