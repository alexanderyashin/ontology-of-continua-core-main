## Repair Rule

For every refuted strong claim, 026 must record:

- the old strong claim;
- the 025 refutation reason;
- the repaired claim;
- the proof obligation;
- the proof or rejection artifact;
- the article boundary consequence.

If a claim can be made true only by adding an arbitrary axiom, it is rejected
from core rather than promoted.
