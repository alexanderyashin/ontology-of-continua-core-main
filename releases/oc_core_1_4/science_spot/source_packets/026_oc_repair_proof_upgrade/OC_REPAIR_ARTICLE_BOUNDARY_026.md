# OC Repair Article Boundary 026

## Allowed Thesis

OC Core may be presented as a bounded formal theory of description repair under
unresolved constraint pressure.

The article may claim:

- the repaired central schema is proved under explicit trigger conditions;
- the source operators are role-preserving semantic operators with local
  independence under typed effect contracts;
- K0-K12 is a reference ladder with declared adjacent invariants;
- law-like OC statements require scope conditions and falsifiers;
- domain projection requires local observables, operators, falsifiers, and
  evidence.

## Forbidden Thesis

The article must not claim:

- universal dimension/collapse law without trigger;
- global minimality or uniqueness of the six operators;
- unique or exhaustive K0-K12 partition of reality;
- crown stones as established consequences without closed dependencies;
- validation of domain projections from the OC core signature alone.

## Current Honest Position

The repaired core is stronger than 024 because it has proof and counterproof
artifacts, and more honest than the old strong model because it keeps every
025 refutation as a boundary. It is a theory in the bounded formal sense, not a
completed empirical law across domains.
