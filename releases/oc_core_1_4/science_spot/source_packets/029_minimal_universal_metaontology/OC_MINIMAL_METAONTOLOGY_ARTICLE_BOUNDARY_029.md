# OC Minimal Metaontology Article Boundary 029

## Allowed Thesis

OC-M is a minimal universal metaontology for observable/articulable reality,
up to definitional equivalence among metamodels satisfying the stated adequacy
axioms.

## Required Wording

| Topic | Article-safe wording |
| --- | --- |
| Universality | OC-M is universal for articulated observation-bound descriptions, not for unobserved facts without modeling. |
| Uniqueness | OC-M is unique up to definitional equivalence among metamodels satisfying the adequacy axioms. |
| Minimality | Removing any OC-M adequacy role or typed operator role breaks a named adequacy witness. |
| Operators | The six continuum operators are minimal under typed semantic effect signatures. |
| Central principle | Structured pressure has four formal outcomes: absorption, extension, collapse, or underdetermination. |
| K-levels | K-levels are closure/rank levels; K0-K12 is a reference instantiation, not an absolute cosmic count. |
| Laws | Law-like OC statements require trigger, scope, outcome criterion, and falsifier. |
| Crown stones | Crown stones are not article-promoted core theorems unless separately derived. |
| Domains | Domain projections require local observables, operators, invariants, falsifiers, and evidence bindings. |

## Forbidden As Promoted Wording

The article must not claim:

- OC proves all concrete domain facts without local evidence;
- every real conflict must birth a dimension or collapse;
- K0-K12 is the only possible hierarchy of reality;
- the operators are globally minimal outside typed semantic effects;
- crown stones are already proved by the minimal core alone;
- alternatives are "worse" without naming the adequacy axiom they fail or the
  redundancy they add.

## Journal-Safe Strong Claim

The strongest current claim is:

OC-M gives a formally checked minimal adequacy core for universal
metaontology. A rival universal metaontology must either reproduce the same
adequacy-role structure up to definitional equivalence, add redundant structure
over that core, or fail one of the formal adequacy witnesses.
