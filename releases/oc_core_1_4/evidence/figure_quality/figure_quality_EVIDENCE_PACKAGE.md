## Acceptance And Refusal

Acceptance requires the chapter sample, source handle, and validation surface to converge on the same limited statement. A reviewer should inspect whether figures are introduced, interpreted, and locally different across history, formal, domain, and evidence chapters. The refusal condition is equally local: It fails if diagrams reuse one generic route or if captions do not explain the scientific payload. A release reviewer can stop at the first missing source, over-strong sentence, or vanished boundary because the package is designed to fail cheaply.
