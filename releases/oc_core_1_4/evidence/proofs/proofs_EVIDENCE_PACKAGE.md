## One-Page Test

Choose one theorem-like sentence and try to rebuild the route from assumptions to conclusion without importing trust from the surrounding prose.

The misuse to watch for is specific: A proof package fails when mathematical language is used as atmosphere rather than as a reconstructable argument. The positive case is also specific: The package is strong when the reader can distinguish proof, proof sketch, finite witness, conjecture, and didactic analogy without guessing. The nonclaim boundary is: It does not say every OC assertion is formally proven; it says proof-bearing passages are labeled and bounded.

## Acceptance And Refusal

Acceptance requires the chapter sample, source handle, and validation surface to converge on the same limited statement. A reviewer should compare one proof appendix, one theorem chapter, and the Lean-facing discussion, checking whether the proof route is reconstructable from named dependencies. The refusal condition is equally local: It fails if a proof paragraph asks the reader to accept an unshown bridge, or if a theorem-like claim lacks its assumptions. A release reviewer can stop at the first missing source, over-strong sentence, or vanished boundary because the package is designed to fail cheaply.
