## Local Inspection Lens

This family is read through **claim strength and modal discipline**. This family checks whether the book's public claims are stated at the right strength: a claim may be explanatory, bounded, theorem-like, empirical, or merely motivational, and each class needs a different support route. For this release candidate, the public handles are K0_CLAIM_REGISTRY, EVIDENCE_SOURCE_2B2C5938E6, RELEASE_SCOPE_CONTRACT; the mapped artifact surface is master monograph; and the first reader samples are abstract: a report on a universal language of system behaviour; acknowledgements; author motivation: from enterprise architecture to a theory of systems; boris stern's no-domain-walls intuition. The family is therefore not a generic PASS page: it is a route for testing one kind of support under one kind of failure pressure.

## One-Page Test

Mark every strong verb in the sampled passage. The package is doing its job only when the verb strength tracks the proof or evidence route visible nearby.

The misuse to watch for is specific: A claim package becomes dangerous when a fluent explanatory sentence quietly stops being conditional. The positive case is also specific: The package is strong when claim wording becomes narrower as the evidence becomes narrower, and when broad language is reserved for carefully marked research programme statements. The nonclaim boundary is: It does not certify a complete theory; it certifies that the release-facing claim register is disciplined enough for owner review.

## Acceptance And Refusal

Acceptance requires the chapter sample, source handle, and validation surface to converge on the same limited statement. A reviewer should sample the introduction, the formal road-map, and one domain chapter, then ask whether each strong sentence names its assumptions or points to a visible reopening condition. The refusal condition is equally local: It fails if a graceful paragraph silently turns a diagnostic row into proof, or if a local example is promoted into a global law. A release reviewer can stop at the first missing source, over-strong sentence, or vanished boundary because the package is designed to fail cheaply.
