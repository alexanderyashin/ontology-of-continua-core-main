# OC Core 1.4 Didactic Example Policy

This policy is normative for candidate 059 and later.

Pedagogical numbers and toy formulae may teach structure, reading order, or dependency pressure. They may not serve as empirical evidence unless bound to a dataset, script, caption, and interpretation record.

Reviewer rule:

- If a number is declared pedagogical, read it as a didactic example only.
- If a number is used as evidence, require a data/script/replay binding.
- If a passage blurs these classes, it is a publication blocker.

This policy closes the class of toy-number leakage by governing the interpretation of every didactic numerical surface, rather than banning individual words.
