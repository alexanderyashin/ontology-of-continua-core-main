# OC Core 1.4 Model Cheat Sheet

## Strict status

OC Core 1.4 is currently best read as a bounded operational metaontology and reproducible research package. It is not yet an independently peer-reviewed empirical theory or universal law.

## Core vocabulary

- Continuum: a trackable range of variation.
- Boundary: a rule or condition that separates what belongs to the described configuration.
- Operator: a transformation that changes or preserves structure under stated conditions.
- Projection: a disciplined transfer of description across surfaces, with preserved and lost structure declared.
- Collapse: loss of the organization that made the configuration legible under the present description.

## Claim ceiling

The central hypothesis is a research-guiding framework claim unless a local claim earns stronger support. Stronger support must be formal proof, replay validation, empirical evidence, or independent replication.

## Failure modes

OC fails locally when it cannot state what varies, what changes, what boundary applies, what evidence binds the claim, or what would count against the projection.
