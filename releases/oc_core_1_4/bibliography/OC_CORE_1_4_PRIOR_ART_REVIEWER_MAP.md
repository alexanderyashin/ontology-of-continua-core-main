# Prior-Art Reviewer Map

The public prior-art story has three layers.

## Direct comparators

These constrain the claim language most directly: systems theory, cybernetics, complexity theory, autopoiesis, effective complexity, network science, percolation, scaling and renormalization, self-organization, and information/predictability frameworks.

## Neighboring theories

These provide local pressure but not authority transfer: thermodynamics, synergetics, RAF/autocatalytic networks, institutional theory, formal methods, and reproducibility/artifact-review practice.

## Non-comparable inspirations

These may motivate language or caution but do not prove OC: broad philosophical lineage, analogies across domains, and historical examples of changing descriptions.

## Non-claim

OC does not replace the listed traditions and does not inherit their authority. A comparator either bounds a local OC claim or exposes a place where OC must stay silent.
