# Start Here For Reviewers

OC Core 1.4 is a research package, not a public announcement or DOI submission.

## Fast routes

- Journal editor: read the flagship article, proof-status glossary, SPOT boundary, and model cheat sheet.
- Scientific reviewer: inspect the 48 promoted claims, the proof certificate, the evidence package index, and the prior-art reviewer map.
- Formal/proof reviewer: inspect `proof/claim_registry_022.json`, `proof/proof_closure_certificate_022.json`, and the SPOT formal source packets.
- Reproducibility reviewer: inspect the methods companion, checksums, public data schemas, and Demonstrator tests.
- Demonstrator reviewer: inspect `tools/oc_core_demo`, public data, and runtime QA evidence.

## Boundary

The 48 promoted public claims are the narrow public projection. The 807-row Public Science SPOT is the broader frontier. The two must not be collapsed.
