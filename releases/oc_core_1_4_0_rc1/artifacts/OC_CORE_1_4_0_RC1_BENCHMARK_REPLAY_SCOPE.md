# OC Core 1.4.0 RC1 Benchmark Replay Scope

Status: `NO_SEND_CANDIDATE_ARTIFACT`

This artifact binds the 1.4 forward candidate to a benchmark/replay scope. It does not assert that the suite is complete.

## Replay Rules

- Quantitative or predictive wording requires protocol, code, data or fixture, output hash, tolerance envelope and falsifier.
- If a replay is absent or inconclusive, the claim ceiling is demoted to structural/operator signature or proof obligation.
- IMN/QMT material is competitor signal and baseline material only.
- OC claims must be compared against explicit baselines before stronger support classes are allowed.

## Initial Benchmark Families

- Bayesian and softmax/annealing baselines
- Replicator and active-inference baselines
- Quantum instrument and Lindblad/SME toy baselines
- SPRT and stabilization baselines
- Domain bridge replay cases for physics, chemistry, biology and K8 systems

## Remaining Gates

The benchmark suite remains a forward blocker for 1.4 publication readiness until fixed seeds, data manifests, plot manifests, tolerance envelopes, no-signalling checks, baseline comparisons and automatic claim-demotion behavior are present.
