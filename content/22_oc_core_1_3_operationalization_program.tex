\subsection{Why the present release separates explanatory force from predictive promotion}
\label{sec:oc13-prediction-claim-taxonomy-v3}
The present monograph is written under a strict rule: explanatory or integrative force is not itself a license for positive empirical promotion.
A claim is allowed into positive outward science only when it is source-bound,
proof-bound, dependency-exact, and, for empirical domains, clears the
source-level empirical promotion bar: exact-match deterministic statements,
theorem-native trace, observable binding, pinned external-data route, numerical
packet, source-owned domain packet, numerical parameter law, locked held-out
replay procedure, held-out results that clear the declared
five-sigma/no-tail-breach threshold, comparator audit,
source/observable/comparator/falsifier agreement, and a replayable falsifier.
Release-wide promotion is a second gate on top of
that empirical bar: the science atlas must be PASS, the integrability suite must
be PASS, all K0--K12 rows must be present, bridge-only residual containment must
not be used as promotion evidence, bridge-only promoted domains must be zero,
and hostile-review blockers must be zero.

This is why the release now carries a distinct operationalization stack. It records what is already closed, what is only bridge-level, what has an official external benchmark route, and what still blocks a lawful platinum release.

\subsection{Domain order and promotion rule}
The operationalization order is fixed rather than opportunistic: mathematics
first as the validated formal anchor; then physics, chemistry, biology, and
systems/civilizational projection as theorem-native domain lanes whose evidence
routes are empirical, replayed, and bounded. Metaontological and refuted draft
lanes remain supporting or excluded rather than promotional.

\subsection{Interpretation}
The table is meant to keep the monograph honest. A domain may be conceptually projected by the OC hierarchy without yet being promoted as a standalone predictive theory.
For this reason the current release distinguishes bridge status from hard closure and treats missing numerical or replay layers as publication blockers for platinum empirical claims.

\subsection{Release consequence}
The foundational consistency dossier therefore records a scoped promotion for the five domain lanes reviewed in this release.
This paragraph is a reduced synopsis; the full per-domain validation tables carry the residual counts and replay metrics. The matrix records each lane as aligned only within the declared evidence boundary. Any future empirical lane that
lacks a standalone source-owned domain packet, theorem-native trace, observable
binding, pinned data route, exact-match deterministic statement, numerical
parameter law, locked held-out replay procedure, held-out five-sigma/no-tail-breach replay,
comparator audit, agreement across source, observable, comparator and
falsifier rows, or replayable falsifier remains
outside promotion by policy; the guard is a standing publication boundary
rather than a present defect in the five lanes promoted here.
This does not turn OC Core into an unrestricted all-domain empirical theory; it
states that the promoted kernel, theorem spine, domain packets, deterministic
statements, held-out evidence, comparator rows, source/observable agreement,
and replayable falsifiers are mutually aligned under the declared promotion
bar.
