\section{Hybrid-Escalation Evidence Bar and Execution Protocols}
\label{sec:oc-core-1-3-empirical-execution-protocols}

The present release does not treat empirical strength as a substitute for theorem-native closure. A domain can be promoted only after the source theorem route, observable binding, numerical packet, held-out replay, comparator audit, and falsifier conditions all agree inside the declared scope.
The evidence bar is \emph{hybrid escalation}: pinned official or open primary data are required first; institute-run measurement or observation waves remain standby contingencies triggered only when the declared open-data route is insufficient, under-covered, or breached. They are not a rhetorical shortcut around the theorem-native trace.
Canonical provenance for the matrix below is the source-owned science surface
\occode{releases/oc_core_1_3/editorial/OC_CORE_1_3_SCIENCE_SPOT_latest.json}
and the domain bundle registry
\occode{releases/oc_core_1_3/editorial/OC_CORE_1_3_DOMAIN_CLOSURE_BUNDLE_REGISTRY_latest.json};
the per-domain bundle files remain the authority for launch rules, transition
gates, selected route institutions, and replay routes.

\subsection{Global evidence bar}
For empirical lanes the default statistical bar is now fixed in machine-readable form: minimum cases `30`, coverage ratio `1.0`, mean absolute normalized error at most `1.0 sigma`, `p95` at most `2.5 sigma`, `max` at most `5.0 sigma`, tail breaches `0`, severe residual `1.5 sigma`, and critical residual `2.5 sigma`.

These quantities are not decorative, but they are also not the whole promotion rule. They operate beneath the theorem-native trace: a numerical pass cannot promote a claim whose source, proof, observable, comparator, or falsifier bindings are missing or wider than the declared lane.

\subsection{Per-domain escalation doctrine}
\paragraph{Mathematics.}
Protocol id: `OC13::EXECUTION::MATHEMATICS::ANCHOR\_REPLAY`. Evidence bar: `HYBRID\_ESCALATION`. Current execution readiness: `PASS`.
Pinned route families: Clay Mathematics Institute (OFFICIAL\_PROBLEM\_CATALOG); American Institute of Mathematics (OFFICIAL\_PROBLEM\_CATALOG).
Held-out policy: `DETERMINISTIC\_COUNTEREXAMPLE\_AND\_TRACE\_REPLAY` with minimum cases `31`.
Quantitative gate: minimum cases `31`, strictly terminalized cases at least `31`, counterexample survivor total at most `0`, and replay divergence total at most `0`. Coverage, residual sigma, Brier, ECE, and tail-breach metrics are not applicable to this exact replay lane.
Replay harness: `logion/k7/spe/orchestrator/science/run\_claim\_simulations\_v1.py` with deterministic replay required `True`.
Institute-run trigger: `ONLY\_IF\_DETERMINISTIC\_REPLAY\_BREAKS`. Measurement wave: `NONE`.
Escalation plan: Expand the exact-question corpus instead of broadening claims..
