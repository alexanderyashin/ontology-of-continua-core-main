\begin{abstract}
Core 1.3 is the flagship master monograph of the Ontology of Continua. It
presents a continuum-based framework organized through the K-level stack,
operator shell, threshold landscape, collapse and rebirth grammar, and bounded
domain packets for mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, and systems. The
volume is written as one scientific object rather than as a short article plus
hidden technical spillover, so the reader can inspect the theory, its closure
rules, and its practical consequences inside a single canonical source.

The manuscript follows a deliberate teaching and audit order. The opening
chapters introduce the problem, model, result contour, and discussion. The
formal core then fixes ontology, thresholds, operators, levels, branching, and
falsifiability. The Core 1.3 closure block states the publication boundary,
foundational consistency, theorem roadmap, worked examples, operational and
empirical promotion discipline, proof machinery, TOE synthesis, and practical
use. The appendices keep the full audit, atlas, and support corpus inside the
same volume without forcing the central reading path to follow raw directory
order.

The scientific posture of the release is intentionally sober. Internal
theorem-native claims, empirical bounded claims, and practical or comparative
claims are kept distinct. Practical utility is shown only where the closed
packets, replay surfaces, and falsifier discipline already support it; frontier
programmes and hypothesis-only rows remain visibly separated. The monograph
therefore does not ask the reader to trust scale, rhetoric, or aspiration. It
asks the reader to inspect a structured scientific object whose proof,
benchmark, and practical-use surfaces remain synchronized under one authority.

Core 1.3 also makes its publication logic explicit without letting that logic
dominate the scientific voice. The source-first discipline, theorem-fate
separation, reviewer-navigation route, and relation to narrower journal-core
derivatives are all stated, but they are subordinated to the primary role of
the book: to serve as the stable scientific reference for review, teaching,
comparison, and external use.
\end{abstract}
