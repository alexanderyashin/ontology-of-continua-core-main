\begin{abstract}
Ontology of Continua (OC) Core~1.3 is a single reference volume for a theory
of continua: structured objects that arise, persist, collapse, leave residue,
and sometimes are later reborn. The problem it addresses is practical as well as formal: how
to compare such structures across domains without turning a shared vocabulary
into an unrestricted prediction claim.

The monograph contributes a bounded public account of that theory. It teaches
the formal core, records the defended proof route, reports the empirical
results that meet the stated evidence criteria, and states what can now be
used in practice. It keeps theorem-native claims, bounded empirical claims,
and practical or comparative claims visibly separate.

The limit is explicit. This volume does not claim unrestricted prediction or
replacement of local scientific models. It offers a large, inspectable,
graduate-level monograph whose main body teaches the defended theory. Its
appendices preserve the evidence record, the benchmark apparatus, the synthesis
support materials, and the practical-comparison archive needed for hostile
review and bounded external use.
\end{abstract}
