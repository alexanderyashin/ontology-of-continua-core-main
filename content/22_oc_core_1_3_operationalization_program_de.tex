\subsection{Prediction claim taxonomy after external review}
\label{sec:oc13-prediction-claim-taxonomy-v3}

The word ``prediction'' is now tiered. A row may use only the strongest tier for
which it has evidence.

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.12\textwidth}p{0.24\textwidth}p{0.54\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
Tier & Name & Required support \\
\midrule
\endfirsthead
\toprule
Tier & Name & Required support \\
\midrule
\endhead
P0 & no predictive authority & Vocabulary, interpretation, or frontier notation only. \\
P1 & structural signature & Operator/threshold pattern with declared observables, but no generated numerical value. \\
P2 & diagnostic classifier & A rule that classifies a known case, with input conditions and falsifier. \\
P3 & scenario or replay lane & Pinned data route, target variable, split policy, code or exact procedure, output, comparator, and falsifier. \\
P4 & held-out quantitative prediction & All P3 supports plus a predeclared value or interval evaluated after lock. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

Core~1.3 may claim P1/P2 structural or diagnostic content broadly. It may claim
P3/P4 only for rows that actually print the protocol, data/code route, output,
comparator, and falsifier. Physics and systems/civilization rows therefore do
not become OC-generated point predictions merely because a benchmark or
residual table exists elsewhere in the release.
